# Problem Statement & Product Vision

## The one-line

A multi-tenant SaaS platform where gym organisations run their coaching business:
instructors build structured, versioned training programmes; members execute them
(including offline); coaches monitor and adjust; and an AI assistant operates inside
explicit, instructor-defined guardrails.

## Why this exists

Most "fitness apps" are a workout log wrapped around an LLM. That is a shallow product.
The durable value here is the **coaching operating system**:

- Instructors author programmes with real structure (mesocycles, progression rules,
  deloads, conditional alternatives).
- Head coaches review instructor work before it reaches members.
- Members get individual or group plans, execute them, and record performance — even with
  no network.
- The platform accumulates **longitudinal, structured training data** that compounds in
  value and powers safe recommendations.

The chatbot is the most talkative doorway into that system. It is not the product.

## Users & their surfaces

| User | Primary surface | Core need |
|------|-----------------|-----------|
| Platform owner | Admin/back-office | Operate the platform, onboard gyms |
| Gym administrator | Web admin | Branches, staff, members, memberships, billing, governance |
| Head coach | Web | Review/approve instructor programmes, oversee a branch's coaching |
| Instructor | Web (desktop-first) | Author programmes, monitor athletes, communicate |
| Member | Mobile (React Native) | Today's workout, log sets offline, track progress, ask the assistant |

Three product surfaces (see [architecture.md](architecture.md)):
1. **Member mobile app** — offline-capable execution + progress + assistant.
2. **Instructor web app** — dense, keyboard-friendly, desktop-first programme builder and
   athlete monitoring. *Not* mobile-first forms.
3. **Gym administration surface** — role-sensitive; can share the web app shell.

## What makes this hard (the real requirements)

- Instructors creating structured, versioned programmes.
- Members on individual or group plans.
- Head coaches reviewing instructor work (approval workflow).
- Gym-level exercise libraries and templates with governance.
- Fine-grained visibility across organisations and branches — one person can be a member
  at Gym A, an instructor at Gym B, and covering a class at Branch C simultaneously.
- Versioned workout programmes with immutable published versions.
- Long-running AI recommendation jobs.
- Auditable changes to health-adjacent recommendations.
- Offline workout recording with sane conflict resolution.
- Potential future wearable / live-session integration.

This is why the backend is the core product, not CRUD around an LLM.

## Product principles

1. **Deterministic domain, talkative interface.** Workout logic is testable code; the AI
   proposes, deterministic rules validate.
2. **Instructors are in control.** The AI's authority is explicitly granted and bounded
   per gym / instructor / member.
3. **History is sacred.** Published prescriptions and performed sets are immutable records.
   You never silently rewrite what a member was meant to do or did.
4. **Tenant isolation is a safety property**, enforced in the application and reinforced
   by the database.
5. **Offline is a first-class mode**, not a degraded one, for members.

## Non-goals (for now — explicitly out of scope)

- Kafka / event-streaming backbone (Postgres outbox + queue is enough early).
- Database-per-tenant (only for very large enterprise / contractual isolation later).
- gRPC to the mobile client.
- Next.js / server-rendered web (no meaningful SEO surface for the authenticated
  workspace).
- Autonomous AI that mutates programmes without validation or, where required, coach
  approval.
- Pose estimation / movement analysis / wearables in v1 (kept in mind architecturally,
  built later).
- Personalized medical, injury-rehab, or financial advice generated by the AI without
  instructor authority.

## Success looks like

- An instructor can build a multi-week programme, submit it for review, publish it, and
  assign it — with full version history.
- A member can complete a logged workout on the subway with no signal, and it syncs
  cleanly and idempotently later.
- An AI-proposed session adjustment is either auto-applied within guardrails or routed to
  a coach for approval — and every such decision is auditable.
- No cross-tenant data leak is representable through the normal repository API.
